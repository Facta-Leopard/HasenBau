# HasenBau website

Use the app's existing paper-art identity: cream stock, dark green ink, terracotta accents, its original rabbit-house icon and subtle hanji fibers. The site is a quiet editorial companion for support and privacy, not a dashboard. Korean and English appear together within every section; never hide either behind a language switch.

Use a serif wordmark and headings, readable Korean sans-serif body, generous margins, shallow layered paper shadows, thin rules and 44px minimum link targets. Keep body text at least 16px. At narrow widths, collapse to one column and wrap navigation without horizontal overflow. All content and navigation work without JavaScript. No external fonts, analytics, forms, ad scripts or cookies. Decorative texture must not compromise contrast. Support keyboard focus, reduced motion and print.

Assets are copied unchanged from the HasenBau app: owner-supplied BrandIcon and generated PaperHanji. No new third-party UI package or font is used.
